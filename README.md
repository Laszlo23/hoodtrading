## The story

The Robin Hood story is a name we borrowed. Each step is a real page. Hood Street kept the street.

1. **The wood.** The forest is the name. The app is one coin, one pool, and a ledger anyone can read.
2. **The coin.** $HOOD was minted once. One billion tokens. No tax, and no second mint.
3. **The bow.** The bow draws when your wallet signs. The desk does not place the order.
4. **The pool.** One market: $HOOD for WETH. Each swap pays 1%, and the fee stays in the position.
5. **The seed.** Bring ETH. The treasury meets you in the pool. 2% of that ETH goes to the cause.
6. **The book.** The ledger lists every swap the chain already wrote.
7. **The street.** Notes are what Hood Street said.
8. **The pack.** DogiHood is [dogihood.com](https://dogihood.com/). CCFF00 is Hood Street. Seeder and Inner Circle are the Night Ledger book.

The stills live on [Night Ledger](https://doghood.aibusiness.fun/). This desk shows them in one line above the book.

## This book

- **Trade.** ETH and tokenized stocks, chart, book, and ticket.
- **xHOOD.** Stake HOOD. Rewards stay in the pool, so the rate climbs. Unstaking burns xHOOD and returns the HOOD it claims. Stake APR on the desk is 12%.
- **Vault.** A deposit leaves the perps balance and becomes USDG shares. Vault APR on the desk is 6.4%.
- **Points.** Stake, vault, volume, and a referral. One hundred points claim one HOOD.
- **Share.** A desk that opens with your code picks up 50 points. The public link is `https://hoodtrading.aibusiness.fun/join/YOUR-CODE`.
